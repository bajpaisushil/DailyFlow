Eight marks to choose from

Every mark is drawn on the same 100 × 100 grid with a single stroke weight, so they can be compared fairly. Pick by number.

Judge them small, not large. A home-screen icon is seen at about 36 px. Each card shows the mark at full size, then at 56 px and 36 px on light and dark tiles — if it survives the small row, it works.

Day Arc

The day as an open circle, with a dot marking where you are in it. The gap says the day isn’t finished — which is the whole idea of the app.
